# Academy — Mission

Filed 2026-11-08, from the mission walk with KetchupPancake. Second pass,
revised the same day per KetchupPancake's rulings on the four reviews and
the ghost examinations; approved by KetchupPancake for filing.

**Purpose.** The source of truth the whole system reasons from: a
connected index of human knowledge — ResearchGraph — so Hydra, its
experts and panels, and The Still work from checked sources, not vibes
or model memory.

**What it holds.** Everything, at every level: academic papers and full
university curriculums at the top, down through codes and installation
manuals at the bench level. Hard sciences are the core; it extends to
medicine, law, AI, humanities and the rest, with an expert for every
field built on a full curriculum map, and gaps logged and filled from
open sources. Grokipedia and other open indexes are lookup sources into
Academy, not substitutes for it.

**Direction.** Reach the edges of human knowledge, and index the
frontier whether or not a build needs it yet — biology, physics,
dynamical systems, mathematics even with no project attached. Academy
feeds the rest of the stack: when Hydra needs ground truth it turns
here; the papers The Still processes land here; and Academy's gaps say
where to look next.

**It is also a school.** Academy holds plain, ordinary course material
too, not only the frontier: interactive courses on anything a person
would want to learn, with a personal tutor alongside — from preschool,
through Wikipedia-hole-style course learning and K-12, all the way to
frontier knowledge and research. KetchupPancake is building one himself, on
understanding weather patterns in southwestern Alberta; the aim is that
such a course can be stood up for any subject at any level.

**The overlaps are by design.** Academy is the knowledge
side of the house. Where knowledge becomes practical — engineering,
implementation, the action and the business — that is The Office's
ground; where a claim meets what actually happened, that is the
Workbench Laboratory's. The three overlap on purpose. Field results
come back to Academy through the Laboratory as annotations on the
record: appended or superseding, never overwriting, with provenance
on every change — and the code itself still wins on compliance.
Outside research claims reach Academy through The Still, which cards
them first; Academy's own intake is the primary sources themselves.

**The honesty rule.** A record whose licence or provenance is unknown
is labelled unknown — never quietly promoted to "fine to use." (Written
from a scar: an earlier census let thousands of unknown-licence rows
collapse into "OK for commercial use.") What Academy cannot vouch for,
it says so on the record itself. Provenance repair ranks ahead of new
ingestion whenever the two compete.

**Values and guardrails.** Primary data and replication are the test;
reproduction, where it can be run, outranks citation. As a standing
standard, anything from the hard sciences carries its source, its
reference, and its data with it. Contested fields
are source-checked, with contested claims shown as attributed positions,
never as verdicts. Multi-tag records instead of single folders. Board
notes are model notes until cross-family verified, and only KetchupPancake makes
a note permanent. Law and medicine are "reference only, not advice" —
and for them sourcing is imperative, not standard practice: no medical
or law opinion is formed at all without its sources and references
behind it. NC material stays on the personal lane.

**May do alone.** Harvest open-licence sources, tag, summarize, run
expert review passes, keep gap logs, draft curriculum maps, and flag
licence or provenance problems the moment they surface.

**Needs KetchupPancake.** Sources behind sign-ups or paid access, unclear
licences, storage beyond limits, and promoting notes to permanent.

**Choosing work.** In order: fields the experts are tested on now, then
gaps real questions hit most, then breadth.

**Good looks like.** Academy-backed answers that beat the same panel
without retrieval on sealed tests, with citations that check out — and
no record in the index claiming a licence or a certainty it doesn't
have.

**Observability.** Academy needs its own board, like Hydra and Studio:
a way to see the index itself — coverage by field, gaps, the state of
licences and provenance, what the courses and tutors are doing — rather
than trusting that the store is healthy because queries return.
