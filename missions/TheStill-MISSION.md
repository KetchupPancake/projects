# The Still — Mission

Filed 2026-11-08, from the mission walk with KetchupPancake. Second pass,
revised the same day per KetchupPancake's rulings on the four reviews and
the ghost examinations; approved by KetchupPancake for filing.

**Purpose.** The pipeline over the newest external research. Papers,
posts and findings come in; what comes out is what they mean for
everything being built here — distilled, checked, and mapped against
the projects, so the frontier's movement lands as usable direction
instead of a reading pile. It is where outside claims earn the right
to influence the system: nothing external moves a wall, a theme, or
a project until it has survived The Still.

**How it works.** Each paper becomes a card: verified against its
source, mapped to the Frontier Map's walls and themes, with
take-aways that say how the paper relates to all the projects. From
the corpus come the Moves and Tests — the consolidations the
take-aways, stacks and gaps point at. A test is a change plus an
eval; tests are also for verification, really for anything
interesting worth running and measuring. Cards are written in one
round and reviewed in a second by a different model; nothing is
published until KetchupPancake checks it. Night Shift can carry the pipeline
overnight once everything is working properly. The tests it
proposes run as evals in Hydra or are executed by Night Shift, and
their verdicts are kept by the Workbench Laboratory. What it cards
lands in Academy's index, and research intakes for Academy and
Studio route through here first. The Still keeps the Frontier Map:
the wall and theme definitions it maps to live in
/00-BRIEF/FRONTIER-MAP.md under its custody.

**Good looks like.** Every paper KetchupPancake posts returns as a card he can
trust — claims checked, licence kept to what the source shows — and
the corpus keeps answering the standing question: what does the
newest research say we should try, test, or stop believing?
