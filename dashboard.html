Advancement candidates — claims that might move a wall

A different kind of candidate. A candidate wall is a question we haven’t named yet; an advancement candidate is something the world shipped — a model, a training procedure, a tool — that might have moved a wall we already have. It lives in a ladder beside the map, not as a card grade: claim → candidate → advancement, with refuted as the exit when the buzz collapses. Promotion is KetchupPancake’s act, never automatic, and it can come through either door of the hybrid bar settled Nov 8, 2026 — the Measured door (our own pre-registered Hydra eval, run through the Workbench Laboratory) or the Waves door (a ground-shaking arrival the field has already confirmed on its own evals). A Waves promotion is marked awaiting our measurement until a Hydra eval confirms it. The full register, tests, and doors are in The Still — Corpus → Advancements.

    Advancements — when the frontier moves on its own

    Papers sharpen a wall a fraction at a time. Moves and Tests are what we do about the corpus. An advancement is the third thing: the frontier moving under its own power — a new model, training procedure, or tool the industry ships — whether we act or not. The concept and the hybrid promotion bar were settled Nov 8, 2026: the concept note and four independent seat analyses (Codex, Claude, Gemini, Grok — all ADOPT WITH CHANGES) are filed in Dropbox at /01-INBOX/ADVANCEMENTS-20261108-muse.md and its sibling analysis notes. An advancement is not a fourth card grade; it is an event recorded in a ladder beside the Frontier Map, pointing at the cards and evals behind it.

    STEP 1ClaimEnters as vendor news does today: a card, graded claim only, wall and theme tagged. Buzz lives here, harmlessly.
    STEP 2Advancement candidateContact plus independent evidence both hold (tests below). Flagged to KetchupPancake in a batch — nothing on the map moves yet.
    STEP 3AdvancementPromoted by KetchupPancake through either door of the hybrid bar. The wall’s movement is recorded with the advancement cited as its cause.
    EXITRefutedPositive disproof only — a gamed benchmark or a failed reproduction that settles it. Not yet reproduced stays at awaiting. The card stays, marked refuted.

    Identifying one — three tests, in rising order of strength

    A candidate needs the first two. The third is the strongest evidence we can hold — and it is what the Measured door runs on.

    TEST 1Contact

    The arrival touches a named wall. Matching a wall’s breakthrough list is a priority signal, never a gate — a strong arrival that matches nothing takes the surprise route, toward the candidate-theme and wall-revision ladder, instead of being turned away.

    TEST 2Independent evidence

    Someone other than the vendor has run it, to an artifact standard: a named operator, an eval disjoint from the vendor’s, and no commercial tie. Ten reports repeating one result are one evidence lineage, not ten.

    TEST 3Our own measurement

    Adopt it in a verified playground and run the eval in Hydra — a new procedure in a drill, a new model in the lanes. Verdicts are kept by the Workbench Laboratory (confirmed / probable / refuted / awaiting); flagging a candidate can spawn its verification test there.

    The hybrid bar — two doors to promotion (ruling #2, settled Nov 8, 2026)

    We need a way to measure and apply what we have — and a ground-shaking release can arrive tomorrow, usually making waves loudly enough to tell. So there are two doors, and promotion through either is KetchupPancake’s act, never automatic.

    The Measured door

    The ordinary path — for the ninety percent: solid arrivals that need proving.

    • A pre-registered Hydra eval, where the wall is measurableRegistered before it runs, with the margin it must clear stated up front.
    • The eval clears its stated marginPromotion cites the measurement itself — the eval, not the announcement.
    • Where a wall cannot be measuredBroad external replication can stand in — the tier follows the wall’s measurability.

    The Waves door

    For the ground-shaking ones — “making waves” with a checkable shape, so it cannot become a hype loophole.

    • Unaffiliated groups confirm it on their own evalsNot the vendor’s numbers repeated — independent runs, independent lineages.
    • Other serious labs visibly reactAdopting it, racing to match it, moving roadmaps because of it.
    • Hostile scrutiny takes its best shot and fails to dent itAnd the arrival lands on a wall’s breakthrough territory.
    • Marked awaiting our measurementUntil a Hydra eval confirms it, the map knows this one was taken on the world’s word.

    How a confirmed advancement folds into the map

    • Recorded in an append-only per-wall movement logDate, claim, criterion touched, evidence, and Laboratory standing. Wall status is derived from that log — and can settle back to standing in quiet periods — instead of being a hand-flipped bit. That stale-bit failure is separate from advancements: W12’s status lagged its own paper tallies, and no advancement mechanism would have caught it.
    • The wall records its cause“This wall moved, <month>: <the advancement>, evidence <the eval>.” Every flip gets a citation, the way a stack of papers earns one paper at a time.
    • Dependent Moves and Tests get reviewedA confirmed advancement triggers a review of the Moves and Tests that depended on that wall — they were planned against ground that just shifted.
    • Movement can run backward, and can accumulateA wall proven harder than we thought is frontier movement too. And a wall shifted by a stack of moves-grade papers can be declared an advancement — vendor waves are not the only thing that counts.
